Why SABC, Sentech & Treasury Are Stuck Together

It’s one broken funding model from the 1990s. Fix one part, the whole thing collapses.

SABC

Losing: R17M/month

Problem: Must fund public mandate with ads + 10% TV licence collection. Can’t pay Sentech.

Sentech

Owed: R1.2B by SABC

Problem: Debt grows R70M/month. Can’t maintain towers or buy diesel. Needs bailout too.

National Treasury

Funds: R100M/year vs R800M needed

Problem: Can’t do ad-hoc bailouts forever. Fiscal constraints + 50 other SOEs asking.

The Circular Debt Trap

  1. Treasury underfunds SABC’s public mandate
  2. SABC uses ad money to cover gap, but ads are falling
  3. SABC can’t pay Sentech
  4. Sentech can’t maintain infrastructure, needs Treasury bailout

The Broadcast Infrastructure SPV Deal

Stop funding failure. Start monetizing assets. Treasury gets equity, SABC gets debt relief, Sentech gets cash.

What Treasury Wins

  • R800M becomes equity in income-generating assets
  • Stops R1B+ ad-hoc bailouts
  • Gets dividends + fiscal credibility
  • 51% control, low risk

What SABC Wins

  • R1.2B debt to Sentech wiped
  • R240M/year guaranteed for public content
  • Split commercial vs public finances
  • Break-even by 2027

What Sentech Wins

  • R480M cash + long-term loan
  • 29% stake in profitable SPV
  • Guaranteed Treasury customer
  • New telco revenue streams

How It Works

Step 1: Move towers + spectrum into SPV owned 51% Treasury, 29% Sentech, 20% SABC

Step 2: Sell capacity to Telcos, Netflix, Gov for 5G, rural backhaul, emergency comms

Step 3: Fund public service from telco revenue, not taxpayer bailouts

Result: 90% rural coverage in 3 years, no retrenchments upfront, BEE capital participation
