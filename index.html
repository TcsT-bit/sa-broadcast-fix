The Broadcast Infrastructure SPV Deal

Stop funding failure. Start monetizing assets. Treasury gets equity, SABC gets debt relief, Sentech gets cash.

What SABC Wins

What Sentech Wins

How It Works

Step 1: Move towers + spectrum into SPV owned 51% Treasury, 29% Sentech, 20% SABC

Step 2: Sell capacity to Telcos, Netflix, Gov for 5G, rural backhaul, emergency comms

Step 3: Fund public service from telco revenue, not taxpayer bailouts

Result: 90% rural coverage in 3 years, no retrenchments upfront, BEE capital participation
